About me

Yasmina Nigmanova — Senior, Marlboro High School

My name is Yasmina Nigmanova, and I am a senior at Marlboro High School with strong interests in leadership, community service, healthcare, and the arts. I have been actively involved in my school through roles such as Student Council Vice President, Executive Board Vice President, founder of the Slavic Society Club, and member of Anytown: Leaders of Diversity, while also completing over 200 hours of volunteer service and gaining experience in childcare and orthodontics. Outside of academics, I am a pianist who performed at Carnegie Hall, a longtime dancer, and a swim team manager who is passionate about leadership, creativity, and making a positive impact in my community.
